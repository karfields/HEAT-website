Mission

Immersive anesthesiology exposure through ethics, skills, mentorship, and research.

H.E.A.T. addresses ethical and technical issues identified in anesthesiology through immersive skills sessions and expert-led conference programming. The conference increases awareness of anesthesiology among medical and pre-health students, highlights research and shadowing pathways, and creates space for discussion about humanitarian and ethical challenges in medicine.
